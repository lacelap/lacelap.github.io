Lacelap

A safe run, right from your door.

Lacelap plans running routes from wherever you're standing: a 5K, 10K, half marathon or marathon, as a loop or an out-and-back. Tap once and get three that start and finish in the same spot, with park paths and sidewalks first and busy roads last.

  • Safety firstSkips overpasses with no sidewalk, back-of-store service lanes, and fast roads.
  • Planned on your iPhoneRoutes are built on the phone from OpenStreetMap data. No account, no server tracking your runs.
  • Share with anyoneFriends open your route in any browser and download the GPX for their watch. No app needed.
